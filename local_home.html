Questions you may be curious about

View All FAQs

Compliance is our operational prerequisite. Every single component placed in our supply chain is traced from origin and accompanied by the required international certifications (CE, RoHS, REACH, UL/CSA where applicable) and the relevant factory test reports. We do not manage secondary or unauthorized sourcing channels: we work exclusively and directly with manufacturers or their official global distributors, eliminating at the root the risk of introducing counterfeit or non-compliant parts.

We know that operational continuity is vital. We constantly monitor product lifecycle through direct communication with manufacturers. When a component enters EOL (End of Life) or receives a Product Change Notification (PCN), our team activates immediately to inform the customer, proposing interchangeable drop-in replacements or planning safety stock to ensure production continuity without line interruptions.

All products distributed by Industrialmac are covered exclusively by the manufacturer's official warranty, under the same conditions, timelines, and procedures established by the manufacturer itself. As a transparent commercial partner, we fully transfer to the end customer every factory protection and warranty against material or functional defects, ensuring that any claim is handled in full compliance with the original supplier's guidelines and timelines.

Our response is based on redundancy and strategic planning. Our supply chain never relies on a single point of failure. We have developed an integrated logistics network using multiple storage hubs and priority transport partners. In addition, for partner companies with established framework contracts, we implement buffer stock and dedicated safety inventory in our warehouse, shielding the customer from fluctuations in international delivery times and ensuring supply regularity.
